# ORIGEN Cultural — Exploration & Wellbeing Model

Status: product principle
Last updated: 6 October 2026

## Core exploration loop
ORIGEN should feel rewarding to explore, especially for Cultural Explorers, but the reward comes from discovering and understanding culture rather than staying online longer.

The Cultural Passport can include:
- countries discovered;
- regions/territories discovered;
- cultural categories collected;
- saved Cultural Providers;
- followed Cultural Providers and Partners;
- cultural stamps;
- thematic collections;
- levels based on diversity of exploration;
- optional cultural quests/challenges.

## Ethical gamification
Use gamification to encourage learning, curiosity and cultural breadth.

Prefer:
- collections;
- passport stamps;
- map completion;
- cultural quests;
- non-punitive levels;
- badges for meaningful discoveries;
- weekly or flexible exploration rhythms.

Avoid:
- rewards purely for minutes spent in the app;
- punitive daily streak resets;
- artificial scarcity designed to create anxiety;
- loss of earned cultural progress because the user took a break;
- dark patterns that make leaving difficult.

A future “streak” may be used only if it is flexible and does not erase progress. For example, active exploration weeks can accumulate without requiring uninterrupted daily use.

## Digital wellbeing
There is no single official evidence-based maximum daily recreational screen-time limit for all adults. ORIGEN therefore uses a transparent product wellbeing target rather than claiming a medical maximum.

Initial product default:
- soft daily wellbeing target: 120 active minutes;
- tracked only while the app is visible/active;
- voluntary reminder, not a hard lock;
- user can take a break, continue 15 minutes, or disable reminders;
- videos pause when the wellbeing reminder appears;
- the daily counter resets on the next calendar day.

This setting is a product default and must not be described as an official medical recommendation.

## Design principle
“Collect cultures, not screen time.”

Progress should be tied to meaningful actions such as saving a Cultural Provider, discovering a new country or cultural category, completing an Academy learning experience, or engaging responsibly with cultural knowledge.
